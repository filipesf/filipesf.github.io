About

I'm Filipe Fernandes, a Design Engineer in Franca, Brazil. I've worked there since 2007.

Get in touch See selected work

From design to frontend

I started in Brazilian graphic design and moved to the web, then learned HTML and CSS to protect the work: I wanted the interface that shipped to keep the intention, rhythm, and detail of the design. By 2013 I committed to frontend because it kept design and implementation in the same conversation, working through product UI, React, TypeScript, and eventually design systems. Choco la Design kept me writing and close to the community around the same question.

International product work

From 2015 to 2025 I worked in Ireland, inside international financial and government clients where a UI decision had to survive regulatory review, distributed teams, and long-lived codebases: Bank of America, Bank of Ireland, Hertz, Walmart, Brazil's Ministry of Health, Brazil's Supreme Court, AnPost, and AA Insurance. The lesson was the same each time: write the component name or the token rule so the next engineer can follow it without asking.

Current focus

Today the work is design systems, product frontend, and AI agent loops. I reach for AI to make a spec or code review faster to check, and stay sceptical of confident output nobody can verify. Find where the Figma file and the codebase disagree, fix it at the token or component level, and write down why.
