About

The through-line is judgement under handoff.

Eighteen years across design, frontend engineering, and product systems taught me to watch the handoff. That is where good ideas usually get diluted.

I learned to protect the decision itself: name the rule, make the safe path obvious, and leave enough evidence for the next person to keep going.
