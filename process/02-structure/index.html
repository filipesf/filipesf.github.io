Structure

The point is to turn research into a shape the team can build from: flows, constraints, data, states, and a few decisions worth defending.

Structure is where discovery becomes usable. I sort the material into flows, states, technical requirements, content needs, data contracts, and open questions. The goal is to make the product easier to reason about before anyone gets too attached to a screen.

This is also where design engineering pays off. A flow that ignores the data model will become expensive later. A component model that ignores the product language will become a UI kit nobody uses. Good structure keeps those decisions in the same conversation.
