Process

I have worked across design and development since 2005: interface work, frontend engineering, design systems, and now practical AI workflows. That mix made me fairly allergic to clean-looking process diagrams that fall apart the moment a real project gets weird.

The shape I come back to is simple: Discover, Structure, Build, and Refine. The order helps, but the work is rarely tidy. Sometimes a prototype exposes the real discovery work. Sometimes a technical constraint changes the structure. The useful process is the one that keeps the team honest while the product is still moving.
